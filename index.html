Lore & Leaks

What we actually know, what's rumoured, and what the bugs on the shore are whispering. Last reviewed November 27, 2026, the day it got a date.

Confirmed Straight from Team Cherry

  • Dec 16, 2025 Sea of Sorrow announced with a teaser on YouTube, the blog and Steam. It's a "nautically themed expansion, coming free for all players," and promises "new areas, bosses, tools, and more!"
  • Dec 16, 2025 The Steam post invites you to "voyage across and beneath the salt-stricken seas." So it's not only on the water but under it.
  • Dec 16, 2025 Team Cherry: "expect additional info shortly before … Sea of Sorrow releases." Remember this one. The release date probably won't come out far ahead.
  • Mar 16, 2026 Patch 5 (1.0.30000) was "intended as the last significant update before Silksong's upcoming major expansion." That's why any new Steam build now gets a MAYBE.
  • May 2026 Fangamer's physical edition listing: the DLC is not on the cartridge or disc and will be a free download.
  • Nov 2026 The physical edition booklet calls Sea of Sorrow the "first giant expansion", lists it as "Coming Soon," and says Silksong "will expand with free content packs". Packs is plural, so more should follow after this one.
  • Nov 27, 2026 🎉 THE DATE: December 11, 2026. Team Cherry's "It's Cherry Time!" presentation dropped the Sea of Sorrow launch trailer: "Voyage above and beneath a vast forgotten sea in Hollow Knight: Silksong's first giant expansion, releasing free for all players on December 11, 2026!"
  • Nov 27, 2026 The same show revealed three games from Team Cherry's friends: The Botanist and the Translator, Radio Rain and Blokkan: Secret of the Walking City.

Not confirmed yet: the exact launch time, a platform list (it still just says "all players"), and the names of any boss or area. Trailer frame-by-frames will sort that out soon.

Rumours Take with a grain of sea salt

  • The monocle emoji (May 2026). Busted Team Cherry's marketing person Matthew "Leth" Griffin answered "Sea of Sorrow this month" with a monocle emoji. Nothing came out in May. Leth is a known chaos agent.
  • "December 31, 2026" on IGDB and game databases. Placeholder As called: it was a stand-in for "sometime in 2026." The real date is twenty days earlier.
  • The "Coming Soon" booklet. Called it The "it's close" crowd wins. Soon meant soon.
  • A slip to 2027. Busted It's still a 2026 release, with three weeks to spare.

Theories Things the community believes

  • Pharloom Bay is back. A seaside area shown in Silksong's 2019 reveal footage never made it into the final game. Many think the expansion is where it went. The lashed, scythe-like lamps in the teaser look a lot like the old Bay footage.
  • The birthplace of Lifeblood. Alchemist Zylotol calls Pharloom Bay "the birthplace of Lifeblood." That makes a sea expansion a natural place for Lifeblood lore.
  • The caged dome. The ornate, wing-finned dome in the teaser could be a diving bell, a sunken shrine or a ship's heart. Nobody knows.
  • Returning cut content. Characters and areas teased before 2025 but missing from launch (fans often bring up Sharpe) are prime candidates.
  • A Hollow Knight-style DLC run. Hollow Knight got four free packs. "Free content packs" suggests Silksong will follow the same plan, with Sea of Sorrow as the big first one.

Patterns How Team Cherry usually drops things

  • Short notice. Silksong's release trailer and date came out August 21, 2025, and the game launched September 4, only two weeks later. And they did it again: Sea of Sorrow was dated November 27 for December 11, exactly two weeks out, same as Silksong.
  • Everywhere at once. Big news usually hits the blog, Steam, YouTube and X within minutes of each other. That's why this tracker can skip X.
  • Betas sometimes come first. Some Hollow Knight content updates went through a public Steam beta branch first. If a new branch appears, the tracker flags it.
  • Quiet until it isn't. Months of silence are normal for Team Cherry. Silence doesn't mean a delay.

Silkpost Spotter

Over six years of waiting turned r/Silksong into a factory of fake news, cursed edits and "THE DATE IS HIDDEN IN THIS IMAGE" posts. The factory is running again for Sea of Sorrow. Here's how to avoid getting got.

The trust ladder

  1. Believe it: teamcherry.com.au, Steam news on the Silksong page, Team Cherry's YouTube and X, and Nintendo, Xbox or PlayStation's own channels.
  2. Probably true: major outlets that link to one of the above.
  3. Something happened: SteamDB changes, rating boards, store listings. Something moved, but that's not a release.
  4. Assume silkpost: Reddit, TikTok, Discord screenshots and "my cousin works at Nintendo."

Red flags

No link

Real news links to Team Cherry or Steam. A post with only a screenshot of a tweet or Steam page proves nothing, since Inspect Element takes ten seconds.

Wrong flair

Check the post flair before the title. Meme, Silkpost and Shitpost flairs mean exactly that. Some silkposters even flair them as "News."

Read the comments first

If the top comment says "silkpost," "you got me" or "SHAW!", it's a silkpost. Real news gets hundreds of "IT'S REAL" replies with links.

CAPS + ???

"IT'S HAPPENING???" or "CONFIRMED?!" with no body text. Real announcements don't need question marks.

Old news, new post

The December 2025 teaser and the Patch 5 "last significant update" line get reposted as new all the time. Check the date.

Placeholder dates

Dec 31, Jan 1 or "Q4" on a database or retailer listing is a placeholder, not a leak.

Leth emojis

A monocle, eyes or wave emoji from Team Cherry's marketing person isn't a date. May 2026 already proved it.

"Nintendo Direct confirmed"

Nintendo announces Directs on its own channels, usually the day before. No Nintendo post means no Direct.

SteamDB isn't a release

A new build or branch means something moved. It could be a hotfix. This site shows it as MAYBE for that reason.

"It's out early!"

The date is real now: December 11. Anything saying it's out sooner, or that it got delayed, needs a Team Cherry link or it's a silkpost.

Hidden-in-the-image dates

Coordinates in the waves, Morse code in the bubbles, a date in the corner pixel. No.

The 5-second test

Open this site. If it says YES, it's real, because YES only shows up when an official post title says Sea of Sorrow is out. If it says NO while Reddit is screaming, you're looking at a silkpost. Or the watcher hasn't checked yet, so give it 5–10 minutes.
